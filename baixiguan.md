Epic Chinese symphonic folk anthem, Xiangxi mountain-river style, anhemitonic pentatonic Zhi mode. Misty intimate opening, rising in waves to a monumental finale. Resonant male baritone and luminous female soprano alternate as leads and unite in duet with layered SATB harmony. Natural children's choir for childhood; rugged low male boatmen call-and-response at raft climax. Dizi, xiao, erhu, dahu, guzheng, suona, strings, horns, low brass, timpani, Chinese dagu, gong, cymbals and bells. 66 BPM rubato opening, 76 chorus, 92 work-song climax, then 52 BPM lament bridge with free rubato and long pauses. Male-led upward key change: baritone and low male chorus in forceful unison, then full SATB in octaves; brass-forward fortissimo, massive crescendo, decisive no-fade ending. Warm, nostalgic, solemn, heroic; clear Mandarin, wide valley reverb, live orchestra and choir, no synths, drum machine, pop groove or autotune.
